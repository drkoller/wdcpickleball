DCPL RFK Partner Tournament

Join us on Sunday, October 4 at the The Fields at RFK Campus for the DC Pickleball League’s (DCPL) RFK Partner Tournament. This is a team-entry doubles event — players must register with a partner. Mixed or same-gender teams are welcome. All players are guaranteed a minimum of 4 games in our round-robin pool plus playoff format.

Format:

Teams in divisions with more than 9 teams will be seeded and separated into multiple pools for round-robin play. The top teams in each round-robin pool will advance to a playoff round to determine the overall winners in each division. Teams in round-robin pools will be ranked according to standard USAP round-robin criteria: number of overall wins, head-to-head wins, overall point differential, then head-to-head point differential.
