# DCPL Preview Design Principles

1. Photography tells the story.

2. The interface frames the story.

3. White space is intentional.

4. Color communicates hierarchy rather than decoration.

5. Blue represents identity.

6. Yellow represents action.

7. Red provides restrained editorial emphasis.

8. Every page should feel calm before it feels energetic.

9. Consistency is more important than novelty.

10. Every design decision should strengthen the DCPL brand.
