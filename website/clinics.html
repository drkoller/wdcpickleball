Learn With DCPL

Clinics

Whether you're new to pickleball or looking to improve your game, DCPL offers lessons and clinics for players of all skill levels. Lessons are led by PPR Certified Coaches Steve Valiant and Maya Ben-David. Steve brings more than 18 years of competitive pickleball experience, including tournament play and medal-winning performances across the United States. Together, Steve and Maya have helped hundreds of players of all ages and skill levels build confidence, improve their skills, and enjoy the game.
