Ladder Leagues

DC Pickleball League offers multi-week ladder leagues throughout the year at multiple locations in the Washington, DC area. Our shootout ladder leagues are designed to let you play multiple games against players of similar skill level in a fun and competitive environment while climbing the ladder throughout the season.
