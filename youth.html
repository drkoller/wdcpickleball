DCPL YOUTH

Programs

DC Pickleball League is working to expand opportunities for young players across the Washington, DC region to learn and play pickleball through clinics, leagues, community events, and tournaments.

For the second year, DCPL collaborated with the DC Police Athletic League (PAL) to introduce pickleball to youth in the District.

Coming October 4 at RFK

DCPL RFK Partner Tournament

On October 4, DC Pickleball League will bring our second annual RFK community event to the Fields at RFK Campus. In collaboration with Go Play’s YSFA (Youth Sports For All) Day, DCPL will host the RFK Partner Tournament, creating 20+ pickleball courts. We’ll be joined by DC Police Athletic League, DC Deaf Pickleball, and many more organizations for a day of pickleball, youth activities, food vendors, and community.
